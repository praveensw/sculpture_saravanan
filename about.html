Who we are

Craftsmanship Rooted in Temple Tradition

We create cement sculptures and decorative architectural elements for Hindu temples – from deity statues and gopuram figures to pillars, mandapams and entrance decoration – made to the design, scale and style each temple requires.

Our work serves temple committees, contractors, architects and individual customers who want sculpture that respects traditional iconography and stands well in the temple setting.

Our approach

What We Stand For

Traditional craftsmanship

Sculpture that follows the proportions, postures and ornament of South Indian temple art, with attention to fine detail and finish.

Custom design capability

Work is made to your reference images, drawings and dimensions. Designs are shared for approval before production begins.

Production and installation

From sculpting in the workshop to transportation and on-site installation, the work is planned as a single project.

Commitment to quality

Each sculpture is inspected before dispatch, and the project is handed over only after your satisfaction at the site.
