Our Process

A clear path from first enquiry to final handover.

Nine steps

How We Work

  1. Customer Enquiry

    Send your enquiry through the form or WhatsApp with a brief of the work you need.

  2. Requirement Collection

    We collect details such as sculpture type, dimensions, quantity, site location and timeline.

  3. Design and Reference Approval

    Designs are prepared from your references and shared with you for approval before work begins.

  4. Quotation and Cost Estimation

    You receive a quotation based on the approved design, size, quantity and installation needs.

  5. Project Confirmation

    The project is confirmed once the scope, quotation and schedule are agreed.

  6. Sculpture Production

    Sculptures are made in our workshop following the approved design.

  7. Quality Inspection

    Each piece is inspected for form, detail and finish before dispatch.

  8. Transportation and Installation

    Finished sculptures are transported and installed at your site.

  9. Project Handover

    The completed work is handed over after final checks at the site.
